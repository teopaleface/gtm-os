# Pricing and packaging

Price around value, buyer constraints, and a testable offer. Public competitor prices are context, not a pricing oracle.

## Steps

1. Define the value event, buyer, budget owner, usage shape, cost to serve, and commercial constraint.
2. Gather current price and packaging evidence from first-party sources and user-provided deal data.
3. Compare two or three packaging options. State who each helps, what it meters, and where it creates friction.
4. Identify the riskiest assumption and design a safe pricing conversation or experiment.
5. State what must be approved by a human before the price changes.

Read `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/scoring.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The output makes a pricing choice easier, labels uncertainty, and includes a low-risk validation step rather than a false precise number.
