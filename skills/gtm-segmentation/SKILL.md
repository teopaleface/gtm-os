# Segmentation

Make segments useful for a decision. A segment needs a meaningful difference in problem, buying motion, value, or reachability.

## Steps

1. State the decision and the segmentation variable. Reject variables that do not change the GTM motion.
2. Define two to five mutually understandable segments with inclusion and exclusion rules.
3. Compare urgency, reachable channels, willingness to change, deal friction, and expansion potential.
4. Mark each input as fact, interpretation, or hypothesis and cite material external inputs.
5. Recommend a focus segment and a deliberate "not yet" segment. Specify what would change the ranking.

Use `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/scoring.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The segments can be identified in practice, the ranking has a reason, and a team knows what to test next.
