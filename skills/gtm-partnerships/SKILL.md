# Partnerships

A partner is useful when both sides can reach a shared customer outcome with less friction.

## Steps

1. Define the shared audience, customer problem, value exchange, and commercial goal.
2. Identify partner candidates and evidence of audience overlap, trust, reach, and operational fit.
3. Choose one partner motion such as referral, integration, co-marketing, or service delivery.
4. Define the smallest pilot, owners, dependency, attribution, and stop rule.
5. Draft the partner brief and list the human approval required before contact.

Use `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/ticket-schema.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The proposal has a mutual customer outcome, a bounded pilot, an evidence gap, and an approval-safe next step.
