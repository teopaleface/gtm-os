# Launch plan

Make the launch a sequence of decisions and learning checkpoints, not a calendar of posts.

## Steps

1. Define the launch outcome, audience, promise, date window, constraints, and non-goals.
2. List the proof required for the promise and the assets needed by each audience.
3. Map pre-launch, launch, and post-launch work to an owner, dependency, channel, and success signal.
4. Add risks, rollback or pause conditions, and the human approvals needed before publication.
5. Choose the first measurement window and the decision it will inform.

Use `../../references/ticket-schema.md`, `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The plan has one outcome, a dependency-aware sequence, measurable checkpoints, and a safe approval boundary.
