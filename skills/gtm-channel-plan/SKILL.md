# Channel plan

Choose channels from buyer behavior and economics, not a list of fashionable tactics.

## Steps

1. State the ICP, buying motion, required trust, expected sales cycle, and available capacity.
2. Compare candidate channels by audience access, intent, message fit, cost, speed, feedback quality, and operational burden.
3. Pick one primary channel and one learning channel for the next cycle.
4. Define the first experiment, asset, owner, time box, and leading signal.
5. List what the plan does not establish yet, especially volume or CAC.

Use `../../references/scoring.md`, `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The plan has a reasoned channel choice, a small test, a measurable signal, and a clear stop or continue rule.
