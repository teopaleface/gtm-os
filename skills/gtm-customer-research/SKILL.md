# Customer research

Ask questions that change a decision. Do not collect opinions without a job for the answer.

## Steps

1. State the decision, target participant, behavior to understand, and what would change the plan.
2. Write a short interview or survey guide focused on recent behavior, alternatives, triggers, friction, and outcomes.
3. Separate direct quotes, observed behavior, interpretation, and sampling limits.
4. Synthesize patterns without erasing disagreement. Note who was not represented.
5. Convert the strongest learning into one ticket, message, or experiment.

Read `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/artifact-contract.md`, and `../../references/scoring.md`.

## Completion

The research has a decision, a defensible sample boundary, evidence tied to claims, and a next action. Do not invent interviews or participant quotes.
