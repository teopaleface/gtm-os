# Competitive intelligence

Study the buyer's alternatives, not just a list of logos.

## Steps

1. Define the audience, buying decision, and comparison criteria.
2. Select a small set of direct competitors, adjacent tools, and the status quo.
3. Capture first-party claims separately from independent customer evidence. Record URL, access date, excerpt, and source independence.
4. Build a comparison table. Mark unknowns instead of filling gaps from memory.
5. Produce a decision-relevant implication: where to compete, where to avoid, and what to verify next.

Read `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/source-adapters.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The table explains a buyer choice, every material claim is traceable, and the output includes a caveat about evidence coverage.
