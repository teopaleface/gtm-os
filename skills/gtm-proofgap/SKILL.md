# Proof gap

Find a useful tension without accusing a company of lying. The result is a validation lead, not a verdict.

## Steps

1. Choose one competitor, one audience, and one promise category such as setup, support, price, or outcome.
2. Capture the exact first-party promise and its URL.
3. Find independent, specific customer experiences that touch the same promise. Deduplicate sources and note dates.
4. Classify the result as aligned, mixed, possible gap, or insufficient evidence. Require at least two independent sources for a possible gap.
5. Draft a careful battlecard entry: promise, customer evidence, caveat, sales question, and next validation action.

Use `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/scoring.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The output never generalizes from a handful of reviews, never says "the competitor lies," and makes the evidence count and diversity visible.
