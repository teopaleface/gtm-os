# GTM experiment

An experiment should reduce one important uncertainty. It is not a disguised task list.

## Steps

1. State the decision, uncertainty, hypothesis, target audience, and time box.
2. Define the intervention, comparison, success signal, guardrail, and minimum useful sample or qualitative threshold.
3. Identify confounders, evidence required, ethical or legal concerns, and the owner.
4. Write explicit continue, change, and stop rules.
5. Record the result and update the related ticket, artifact, and confidence.

Use `../../references/ticket-schema.md`, `../../references/scoring.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

Someone can run the experiment, recognize the result, and make the next decision without guessing what success meant.
