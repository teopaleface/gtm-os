# Content strategy

Make content earn its place in the buying journey. Start from a question, friction, or proof gap the audience already has.

## Steps

1. Define the audience, stage, desired action, and available proof.
2. Collect buyer language and source-backed questions from research or supplied data.
3. Create three to five content angles. Each includes the question, point of view, evidence, format, and CTA.
4. Mark any claim that needs a primary source, customer proof, or experiment.
5. Choose one piece to ship and one signal to measure.

Read `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/artifact-contract.md`, and `../../references/scoring.md`.

## Completion

The content brief is tied to a buyer problem, includes credible proof, and has one measurable next action.
