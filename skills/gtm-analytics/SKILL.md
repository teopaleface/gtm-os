# GTM analytics

Choose metrics because they change a decision. A dashboard without a decision is a decoration.

## Steps

1. State the business question, funnel stage, unit of analysis, and decision owner.
2. Define the event, entity, window, denominator, source, and expected direction for each metric.
3. Separate leading signals, outcome metrics, and guardrails. Name known data quality risks.
4. Specify the smallest instrumentation or analysis needed for the next decision.
5. Add a review cadence and a rule for changing the plan.

Read `../../references/scoring.md`, `../../references/ticket-schema.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The metric definitions are unambiguous, tied to a decision, and honest about missing or biased data.
