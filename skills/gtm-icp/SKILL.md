# ICP discovery

Find the smallest audience with a painful problem, a reachable buying path, and a reason to act.

## Steps

1. Capture the product job, value event, market boundary, deal shape, and known customers.
2. Propose no more than three candidate segments. For each, state problem intensity, frequency, economic impact, access, and likely buyer.
3. Gather evidence for the problem and buying context. Keep customer evidence separate from product claims.
4. Rank candidates with a short scorecard. Show which score is observed and which is a hypothesis.
5. Recommend one first segment, one buyer role, one trigger, and one validation interview or experiment.

Read `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/scoring.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The output names one first segment, one buyer, the reason to start there, the evidence behind it, and the next validation action. Do not call a broad demographic an ICP without a buying problem.
