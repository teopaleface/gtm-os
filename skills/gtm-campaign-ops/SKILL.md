# Campaign operations

Make execution inspectable. Separate drafting, approval, launch, and measurement.

## Steps

1. Capture the approved goal, audience, message, channels, budget, dates, owner, and stop rules.
2. Break work into tickets with dependencies, assets, QA checks, and acceptance criteria.
3. Mark claims, links, targeting, consent, and tracking that need review before launch.
4. Define the launch checklist, rollback path, reporting view, and first review date.
5. Hand off the checklist for human approval. Do not launch or publish from this skill.

Read `../../references/ticket-schema.md`, `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The checklist has owners, blockers, QA gates, measurement, and an explicit approval boundary.
