# Sales enablement

Give a seller a useful decision aid for one audience and one buying situation.

## Steps

1. Define the buyer, use case, alternative, stage, and desired next step.
2. Write the value story in the buyer's language and attach proof to material claims.
3. Add discovery questions, qualification signals, objections, careful responses, and disqualifiers.
4. Include competitor or status-quo context only when it is sourced or clearly labeled as a hypothesis.
5. Add a coaching note and one field signal to review after use.

Use `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/artifact-contract.md`, and `../../references/scoring.md`.

## Completion

The asset helps a seller make one conversation better, shows its proof, and identifies how the team will learn whether it worked.
