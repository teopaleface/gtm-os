# Referrals

Referrals work when sharing helps the customer. Incentives cannot repair a weak value event.

## Steps

1. Define the customer value moment, likely recipient, trust relationship, and desired action.
2. Find evidence of natural sharing, invitations, or recommendations in product or customer data.
3. Draft one referral prompt or flow with a clear benefit and an honest expectation.
4. Set success and guardrail metrics, including abuse, consent, and customer experience checks.
5. Define a small test and ask for approval before sending or changing product behavior.

Read `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/scoring.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The experiment is tied to customer value, has a measurable next action, and treats trust and consent as part of the design.
