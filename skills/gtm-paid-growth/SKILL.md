# Paid growth

Treat paid media as a test of audience, message, offer, and economics. Do not promise CAC from a small sample.

## Steps

1. Define the audience, conversion event, offer, budget cap, time box, and unit economics.
2. Choose one variable to test first and keep the rest stable where possible.
3. Draft ad angles and landing-page requirements from the approved message map.
4. Set success, guardrail, and stop rules before launch. Include consent and policy checks.
5. Record the result and update the next ticket. Any spend or launch requires human approval.

Use `../../references/ticket-schema.md`, `../../references/scoring.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The brief has a bounded spend, a learning question, a measurement plan, and explicit approval before money or public copy is used.
