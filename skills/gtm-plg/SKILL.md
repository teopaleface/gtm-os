# Product-led growth

Map the product behavior that creates value and makes a next user or team interaction natural.

## Steps

1. Define the target user, value event, time to value, product boundary, and commercial goal.
2. Map the loop: discover, start, reach value, invite or share, convert, and expand.
3. Use product evidence for measured behavior and label inferred motivations.
4. Choose one friction to remove and one loop signal to measure.
5. Add privacy, consent, and human approval checks for invitations or public sharing.

Use `../../references/scoring.md`, `../../references/ticket-schema.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The loop has an observable value event, one testable friction, a measurement plan, and a clear safety boundary.
