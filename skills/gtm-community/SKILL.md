# Community-led growth

Earn participation by solving a real shared problem. A list of communities is not a channel strategy.

## Steps

1. Define the audience, shared problem, community context, and value the team can contribute.
2. Research relevant public communities and their rules, language, unanswered questions, and norms.
3. Choose one contribution format and a respectful participation cadence.
4. Define the learning signal and a boundary between contribution and promotion.
5. Draft the first contribution for human review. Do not post automatically.

Read `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/source-adapters.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The plan adds value before promotion, respects community rules, and has a measurable learning action.
