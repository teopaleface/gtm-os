# Market sizing

Make the estimate useful for a choice such as focus, hiring, pricing, or fundraising. A large number alone is not a strategy.

## Steps

1. Define the market boundary, geography, customer unit, time period, and revenue measure.
2. Prefer a bottom-up estimate from reachable accounts, price, and adoption. Use top-down data as a cross-check.
3. Show every input, source, date, unit, and arithmetic step.
4. Run a low, base, and high case. Identify the assumption that moves the answer most.
5. Recommend the cheapest way to validate that assumption.

Use `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/scoring.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

Someone can reproduce the estimate, see its limits, and know which decision it supports.
