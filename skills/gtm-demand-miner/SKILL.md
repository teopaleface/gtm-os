# Demand miner

Find places where the target audience already describes the problem or looks for a solution.

## Steps

1. Lock the audience, problem language, geography, and buying stage.
2. Search communities, reviews, job posts, search patterns, directories, and public product discussions with several phrase variants.
3. Record the source, language used, audience fit, intent signal, and freshness.
4. Group demand by problem, trigger, channel, and level of intent. Do not count mentions as buyers.
5. Recommend one discovery channel and one message test. State the coverage gap.

Use `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/source-adapters.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The result identifies real language and reachable places, distinguishes attention from intent, and gives a bounded next action.
