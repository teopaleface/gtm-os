# Positioning

Positioning is a choice about who the product is for, what problem it owns, and why it is a credible choice now.

## Steps

1. State the first audience, painful alternative, desired outcome, and buying moment.
2. List the product's real capabilities and the proof available for each.
3. Compare the current alternatives, including doing nothing and manual work.
4. Write two or three positioning options using: for, who, problem, category, differentiated value, and proof.
5. Choose one option. Mark unsupported differentiation as a hypothesis and define the next proof check.

Read `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/scoring.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The output contains one positioning choice, the audience and alternative it excludes, proof for the important claims, and a validation action.
