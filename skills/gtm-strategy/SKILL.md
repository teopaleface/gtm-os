# GTM strategy

Choose a sequence of bets. A strategy is not a list of every channel or asset the team might use.

## Steps

1. Define the product, first audience, problem, value event, business goal, time horizon, and constraints.
2. Summarize evidence for the market, ICP, alternatives, message, channel, offer, and operational capacity.
3. Choose the first wedge and the next two decisions it unlocks.
4. Turn the choices into tickets with owners, blockers, evidence needs, and success signals.
5. State what the strategy deliberately does not do and what would change it.

Use `../../references/ticket-schema.md`, `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/scoring.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The brief connects a market choice to an executable sequence, names its evidence gaps, and gives the team a review point.
