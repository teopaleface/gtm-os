# Retention

Retention is repeated value, not repeated reminders. Tie the work to a customer outcome and a cohort.

## Steps

1. Define the retained behavior, cohort, period, contract shape, and baseline.
2. Collect internal usage, support, billing, and interview evidence when available.
3. Segment retained and lost behavior by use case, customer type, and value event.
4. Identify one plausible lever and the assumption behind it.
5. Design a retention experiment with a success signal, guardrail, owner, and review date.

Use `../../references/evidence-policy.md`, `../../references/scoring.md`, and `../../references/artifact-contract.md`.

## Completion

The brief connects retention to observed value, separates fact from cause, and gives a bounded next test.
