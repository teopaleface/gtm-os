## Facts
- SmartBill's page promises automatic SPV delivery, including bulk; its guidance says automation sends to ANAF without intervention but names ANAF infrastructure and expired SPV connection blockers.
- Trustpilot and Apple snapshots report ease/connectivity; Webghid says automation/mass sending starts at Gold while Silver is manual, invoice by invoice.
## Interpretation
The evidence is mixed: the promise and two review domains support low friction, while one independent editorial domain indicates plan-dependent friction.
## Hypothesis
Lower-tier Romanian users may experience the promise as less low-friction than the headline implies; this is a validation lead, not proof of misrepresentation.
## Caveat
The independent plan-tier claim is editorial and unconfirmed by a second independent domain; reviews are directional, not market-size evidence.
## Sales question
For teams sending e-Facturi to SPV, which SmartBill plan and workflow do you use, and where—if anywhere—does manual intervention or plan gating slow you down?
## Cheapest next validation action
Collect two anonymized interviews or public reviews from Silver users specifically covering automated SPV delivery, then compare the workflow with Gold.
## Limitations
Small, non-random snapshots; no controlled test, plan/account verification, or second independent source confirming the plan-tier mismatch; access dates are 2026-08-28.
