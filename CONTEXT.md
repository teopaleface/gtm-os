# GTM OS context

## Purpose

GTM OS helps a founder or growth team move from a fuzzy go-to-market question to a defensible decision and a short list of next actions.

## Vocabulary

- **Request**: the user's current GTM question.
- **Decision**: the choice the work should make easier.
- **Ticket**: one bounded unit of GTM work with a playbook, evidence need, blockers, and acceptance criteria.
- **Playbook**: an explicit-only specialist skill for one GTM job.
- **Evidence**: a source-backed fact with a URL, access date, and short excerpt.
- **Signal**: evidence that a relevant condition may have changed. A signal is not proof of intent, budget, or purchase.
- **Artifact**: a usable output such as an ICP, message map, battlecard, launch plan, or experiment brief.
- **Eval**: a repeatable check for evidence quality, reasoning, actionability, and contract compliance.

## Product boundary

The plugin researches, reasons, drafts, and evaluates. It does not send outreach, write to a CRM, publish a campaign, change pricing, or make a public claim without explicit human approval.
